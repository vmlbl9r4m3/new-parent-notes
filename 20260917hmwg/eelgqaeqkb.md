 IVF is a complex medical procedure that involves fertilizing an egg with sperm in a laboratory setting, followed by the transfer of the resulting embryo(s) into the woman's uterus. For couples with chromosomal abnormalities, preimplantation genetic testing (PGT) plays a crucial role in IVF success. PGT allows doctors to screen embryos for genetic abnormalities before implantation, increasing the likelihood of a healthy pregnancy.
